# IC and metric method catalog

IC methods:

- `ic.spearman_rank`: cross-sectional Spearman/rank correlation;
- `ic.pearson`: cross-sectional Pearson correlation.

Metric methods:

- `metric.rank_ic_mean`, `metric.ic_mean`;
- `metric.rank_ic_std`, `metric.ic_std`;
- `metric.rank_ic_ir`, `metric.ic_ir`;
- `metric.rank_ic_positive_ratio`, `metric.ic_positive_ratio`.

Record signed versus absolute IR convention and sample standard-deviation convention. Normalize printed percentages to evaluator decimal units while preserving original labels and units. Metrics co-reported in one homogeneous result block remain in one truth source.
